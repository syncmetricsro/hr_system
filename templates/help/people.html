{% trans "Adding a worker" %}

{% blocktrans %}Recruiters and Managers can add a new person from the People page. A new record starts without a lifecycle status commitment — it only becomes meaningful once the person is scheduled for a trial or assigned to a project.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Lifecycle statuses" %}

{% blocktrans %}Every worker has exactly one lifecycle status at a time: Available (not currently placed), Trial day (scheduled or completing a trial), Working (actively assigned to a project), Inactive (paused, with a reason on file), or Blacklisted (blocked from re-entry — see the Blacklist article). Status changes happen automatically as part of other actions — scheduling a trial, recording its outcome, activating on a project, or recording an exit — rather than being set directly.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Trial days" %}

{% blocktrans %}Coordinators and Managers schedule a trial day from a person's detail page. After the trial, record its outcome (pass, fail, or no-show) — a pass moves the worker toward activation on the project; a fail or no-show recycles them back to Available so they remain eligible for a future opportunity without losing their history.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Readiness and activation" %}

{% blocktrans %}Before a worker can be activated onto a project, their readiness record needs its required pillars completed (see the Projects article for what counts as a pillar). Activation is blocked with a specific, named reason until every required pillar is satisfied — the error message on the activation button names exactly what's missing.{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Inactive and recycling" %}

{% blocktrans %}Marking someone Inactive requires a reason and keeps their full history intact. From Inactive, a Recruiter, Coordinator, or Manager can recycle them back to Available at any time — this clears the inactive reason but does not erase anything else about the person.{% endblocktrans %}
